A home loan is how most buyers in Ahmedabad complete a purchase. Treat pre-approval as part of shortlisting — not something you start after you have already paid a token.

## Get pre-approved first

Banks and housing finance companies will estimate your eligible amount from income, existing EMIs and credit history. That number should cap your search. A 3BHK in Thaltej and a 2BHK in Gota sit in very different EMI bands.

## What lenders typically ask for

- Identity and address proofs, PAN, and photographs
- Salary slips or ITRs for self-employed buyers
- Bank statements
- Property papers once you have a unit (sale deed chain, society NOC, occupancy certificate)

## Costs beyond the interest rate

Compare processing fees, prepayment charges, and whether the quote is floating or fixed. Stamp duty and registration are paid separately at the sub-registrar — they are not part of the loan amount unless you specifically structure them that way.

<aside class="callout"><p>Never sign a token agreement that assumes a loan will close on an aggressive timeline. Build in time for legal due diligence and lender valuation.</p></aside>
