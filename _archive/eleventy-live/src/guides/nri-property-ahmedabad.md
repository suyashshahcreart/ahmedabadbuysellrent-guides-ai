Many Ahmedabad purchases are made by families who live overseas and still want a home in Gujarat. Distance makes paperwork and local representation the real project.

## Buying from abroad

- Confirm FEMA and eligibility with your banker or lawyer before you pay a token
- Use a registered power of attorney you actually trust for site visits and society paperwork
- Insist on video walkthroughs at more than one time of day
- Budget extra time for attestation and fund remittance

## Renting the unit out

West-side apartments near employment corridors are easier to let than units chosen only for family sentiment. Factor vacancy, maintenance and a local manager.

## Common pitfalls

Unclear society dues, incomplete occupancy certificates, and optimistic possession dates on under-construction projects. Read the [documents guide](/guides/property-documents-ahmedabad/) before you sign.
