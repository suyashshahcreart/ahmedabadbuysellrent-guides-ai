Selling in Ahmedabad requires realistic pricing, clean documentation, and presenting the home well.

## Price realistically

Compare recent sales in your society — not just asking prices on portals. Floor, facing, parking and pending dues all move the number.

## Prepare documents

Original sale deed chain, tax receipts, society share certificate, occupancy certificate, encumbrance certificate, and society NOC.

## List and close

Photograph in daylight, respond quickly to visits, and use a written token agreement. [List on {{ site.org }}]({{ site.marketplaceUrl }}/list) with accurate locality information.
