West Zone is the established west bank of Ahmedabad — older apartment blocks, commercial streets, and campuses clustered between the Sabarmati and the newer corridors of New West Zone.

## Key neighborhoods

- **Navrangpura** — Gujarat University, NID, mixed residential. See [renting in Navrangpura](/guides/renting-navrangpura/).
- **Paldi** — Residential with proximity to Ashram Road and the riverfront
- **CG Road** — High street for shopping and dining. Compare with SG Highway in [SG Highway vs CG Road](/compare/sg-highway-vs-cg-road/).
- **Ashram Road** — Commercial spine connecting the old and new west
- **Ellisbridge, Ambawadi, Memnagar** — Established residential pockets

## Who it suits

Academics and students; professionals who prefer CG Road lifestyle; buyers seeking proven resale markets; renters wanting central west-bank access at moderate rents.
