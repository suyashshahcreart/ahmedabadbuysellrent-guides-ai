These notes are context for readers of our locality guides — not live quotes or forecasts.

## Sale market

Premium demand remains concentrated in New West Zone. Satellite and parts of West Zone offer a step down in ticket size with established amenities. South-East Zone continues to attract first-time buyers who need a lower entry price.

## Rental market

SG Highway employment supports rents in Bodakdev, Thaltej and Prahlad Nagar. Gota absorbs tenants who want newer buildings without those premiums. Navrangpura stays relevant for campus-side and CG Road access.
