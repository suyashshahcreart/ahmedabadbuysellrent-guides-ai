Central Zone is Ahmedabad's historic core — narrow pol lanes, centuries-old gates, and bazaars that still organise daily commerce.

## What makes it unique

Heritage architecture, dense pedestrian lanes, mixed wholesale and residential use, and landmarks such as Sidi Saiyyed Mosque, Jama Masjid and Bhadra Fort.

## Property considerations

- Title complexity and shared ownership within pol communities
- Renovation restrictions in heritage zones
- Limited parking compared with west-side apartments
- A smaller rental market than New West Zone
