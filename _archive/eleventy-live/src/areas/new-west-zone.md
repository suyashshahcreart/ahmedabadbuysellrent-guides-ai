## Lifestyle and amenities

Offices, malls, campuses and gated societies define daily life here. Shopping at Palladium and Ahmedabad One sits alongside lakes and institutes in Vastrapur.

## Connectivity

SG Highway, Metro stations at Vastrapur and Thaltej, and a 20–30 minute run to the airport for many addresses.

## Schools and hospitals

International and premium CBSE schools cluster nearby. Confirm commute from the specific society, not the locality name alone.

## Rental and investment

Strong tenant demand from highway employment. Entry prices are high in Bodakdev and Thaltej; Gota is the value overlay.
