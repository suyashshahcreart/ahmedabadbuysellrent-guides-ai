## Lifestyle

Campuses, CG Road retail and older apartment stock. Quieter residential pockets sit a few streets off the commercial spines.

## Connectivity

Ashram Road, BRTS and Metro access on the west corridor. Central to both the old city and New West.

## Who it suits

Buyers who want proven neighborhoods; renters near universities; households that prefer high-street life over highway towers.
