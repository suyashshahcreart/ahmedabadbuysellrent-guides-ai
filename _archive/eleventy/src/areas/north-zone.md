## Lifestyle

River, ashram precincts and the stadium at Motera. Residential growth continues toward Gandhinagar.

## Connectivity

Roads north, Metro/BRTS on selected stretches, and relatively practical airport access compared with the far south-west.

## Property notes

Mix of older Sabarmati fabric and newer Chandkheda/Motera supply. Visit both for commute reality.
