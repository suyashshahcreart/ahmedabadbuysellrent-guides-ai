## Lifestyle

Large-scale housing, arterial roads and a younger suburban fabric than the west bank.

## Property notes

First-time buyer territory for many households. Check builder track record, RERA status and actual commute to work, not brochure minutes.

See [investing in Ahmedabad](/guides/investing-in-ahmedabad-property/) for yield vs appreciation.
