Photographs hide orientation, noise and finish quality. A 20-minute walkthrough with a simple checklist is worth more than a brochure.

## Layout and light

Stand in each room at the time of day you would actually live there. West-facing glass on SG Highway towers can mean heat. Cross-ventilation matters in older west-zone blocks without central cooling.

## Finishes and services

- Flooring level and grout
- Kitchen wet areas and trap smells
- Bathroom waterproofing clues (stains on the floor below if you can see common areas)
- Electrical load for ACs and geysers
- Covered parking vs. stilt squeeze

## If you plan to renovate

Factor society rules, labour logistics in high-rises, and the months you will pay both rent and EMI. Heritage or pol property in Central Zone has additional constraints.
