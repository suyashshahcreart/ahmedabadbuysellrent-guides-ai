Bodakdev vs Satellite

Compare Bodakdev and Satellite (Jodhpur) on price, commute, schools and lifestyle.

Bodakdev and Satellite are two of the most searched localities on Ahmedabad's west bank. Both sit in New West Zone, but they offer different price points and daily rhythms.

Factor Bodakdev Satellite (Jodhpur)
Character Premium, corporate-adjacent, newer towers Established mid-premium, mixed stock
Price band Higher Lower entry for similar size
Commute to SG Highway offices Excellent Good, slightly longer
Best for High-budget buyers, highway professionals Mid-budget families, first-time west-side buyers

Choose Bodakdev if…

Your office is on SG Highway or in Prahlad Nagar and budget allows a premium 3BHK in a gated society.

Choose Satellite if…

You want west-side living below Bodakdev or Thaltej prices, with local markets and a mixed community.
