Renting in Navrangpura

Tenant guide for Navrangpura — rents, deposits, lease terms and connectivity near campuses and CG Road.

Navrangpura is an established west-bank neighborhood — Gujarat University, NID, and the commercial stretch of CG Road. It attracts students, academics and professionals who want central access without SG Highway prices.

What to expect

  • Mostly older apartment blocks and independent floors
  • 1BHK and 2BHK stock at a wide range depending on furnishing
  • Deposits commonly several months' rent
  • 11-month agreements remain standard

Before you sign

Inspect plumbing and power backup, confirm parking, and ask about society rules. See also West Zone overview and rental listings.
