Living in New West Zone

Area guide for Bodakdev, Vastrapur, Thaltej, Gota and SG Highway — connectivity, lifestyle and who this zone suits.

New West Zone is Ahmedabad's fastest-growing corridor — high-rises, corporate offices, malls, and wide expressways stretching from Prahlad Nagar to Shela. If you are buying or renting on the west side, this is likely where your search starts.

Key neighborhoods

  • Bodakdev — Premium residential with proximity to SG Highway offices. Strong apartment stock, high price band.
  • Vastrapur — Home to IIM Ahmedabad, Vastrapur Lake, and a mix of old bungalows and new towers.
  • Thaltej — Premium corridor with gated communities and international schools. See our Thaltej buying guide.
  • Satellite (Jodhpur) — Established mid-premium area. Compare with Bodakdev in our Bodakdev vs Satellite guide.
  • Gota — More affordable west-side option with newer construction. Popular with renters. See renting in Gota.
  • SG Highway — Commercial and residential mix along the expressway.

Who this zone suits

  • IT and corporate professionals working along SG Highway or in Prahlad Nagar
  • Families seeking international schools and gated societies
  • Investors targeting appreciation in Gota and nearby pockets
  • First-time buyers with budget for mid-range apartments in Satellite or Gota

Connectivity

  • SG Highway connects to Gandhinagar, the airport, and southern Ahmedabad
  • Metro lines serve Vastrapur, Thaltej, and SG Highway stations
  • BRTS Janmarg runs parallel on several stretches
  • Airport is typically 20–30 minutes from most New West localities

Property context

Price bands vary significantly within the zone. Bodakdev and Thaltej command premium rates for 3BHK apartments. Gota and Chandlodiya offer lower entry points with newer stock. Always evaluate the specific society — two buildings on the same road can differ sharply in maintenance quality and resale value.
