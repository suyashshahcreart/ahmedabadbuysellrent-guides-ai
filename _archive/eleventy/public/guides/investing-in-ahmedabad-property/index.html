Investing in Ahmedabad property

How to think about yield, appreciation corridors and risk when investing in Ahmedabad real estate.

Investment in Ahmedabad is still a locality decision first. Yield and appreciation do not move together in every zone.

Appreciation-led corridors

New West Zone — especially pockets with Metro and highway access — has been the story many investors tell. Entry prices are high. Holding periods should be long.

Yield-led pockets

Nikol, Vastral and selected east-side areas can offer more accessible tickets and tenant demand from industrial and logistics employment. Quality of construction and society management varies widely — visit more than one project.

What to underwrite

  • Vacancy and realistic rent, not brochure rent
  • Maintenance as a share of rent
  • Ticket size vs. your liquidity needs
  • Title and RERA status for new supply

Compare this with buy vs rent if you are deciding whether to occupy or invest.
