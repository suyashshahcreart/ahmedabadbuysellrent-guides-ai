Thaltej is one of Ahmedabad's premium residential corridors — gated communities, international schools, and proximity to SG Highway offices.

What defines Thaltej

Large-format gated societies, a mix of builder floors, villas and high-rise towers, Metro access, and a long-term settler profile.

Buyer checklist for this locality

  • Compare maintenance charges across societies
  • Check RERA registration for under-construction projects
  • Evaluate peak-hour commute on SG Highway
  • Visit schools and hospitals you actually plan to use
  • Review society bylaws on future rentals
