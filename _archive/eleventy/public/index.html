Market guides

Premium demand

West-led

Sale prices

New West Zone — Bodakdev, Thaltej and Satellite — continues to command the highest sale prices. South-East and parts of East remain the more accessible entry points for first-time buyers.

Tenant demand

Gota & west

Rental trends

Professionals cluster along SG Highway. Gota and Chandlodiya offer newer stock at lower rents than Bodakdev; Navrangpura remains the established campus-side rental pocket.

City-wide

7 zones

Investment outlook

Appreciation is strongest where Metro, highway access and employment overlap. Yield-focused investors still look at Nikol, Vastral and selected east-side pockets.
